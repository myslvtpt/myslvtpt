Frequently asked questions

MICE basics, answered

What does MICE stand for?

MICE stands for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences and Exhibitions (sometimes the final E is described as Events). It refers to business-focused gatherings and the travel and services supporting them.

Who should consider MICE travel?

Companies, associations, professional communities and organisations planning team meetings, reward trips, conferences, product showcases or trade exhibitions.

When should we start planning?

Start as early as practical. Larger events, peak-season travel, international groups and complex venue requirements usually need more lead time. Confirm supplier availability before announcing final arrangements.

What information should I prepare before requesting a proposal?

Share your preferred dates, destination, approximate group size, event objective, number of nights, budget range, venue requirements and any accessibility or dietary needs.

Can a MICE plan include travel and accommodation?

Yes. Depending on the brief, a plan can coordinate transport, hotel stays, transfers, meeting spaces, event logistics and attendee communications. Confirm the exact scope with your provider.
